## legend

Ivan Tsarevich had a wife who was once a frog, and under the frog skin she was Vasilisa the Wise, the cleverest and most beautiful maiden in the world. But Ivan burned her frog skin too soon, before her spell had run its course, and she vanished from his side. "Look for me beyond thrice nine lands," she told him, "in the thrice-tenth kingdom, with Koschei the Deathless."

Koschei was a sorcerer no sword could kill, for his death was not in his body. He kept it hidden far away, where no one could find it.

Ivan set out after her. Along the way, hungry, he raised his bow at a bear, but the bear begged him, "Don't kill me, Ivan Tsarevich. One day I may be of use to you." Ivan let it go. He spared a drake in the same way, and a hare, and on the seashore he found a pike gasping on the sand and threw it back into the water.

At last an old Baba Yaga told him Koschei's secret. His death was at the tip of a needle. The needle was in an egg, the egg in a duck, the duck in a hare, the hare in an iron chest, and the chest sat high in an old oak, far over the sea on the island of Buyan, which Koschei guarded like the apple of his eye.

Ivan found the oak, but he could not reach the chest. Then the bear came lumbering up, tore the oak out by its roots, and the chest fell and burst open. A hare shot out and ran for its life, but Ivan's hare chased it down and tore it apart. A duck burst out of the hare and flew up high, but the drake dived and struck it, and an egg fell from the duck into the deep sea. Ivan wept on the shore, until the pike came swimming up with the egg in its teeth.

Ivan went to Koschei's castle, broke the egg, took out the needle and snapped off its tip. Koschei raged and thrashed about, but it did him no good: he died. And Ivan Tsarevich took Vasilisa the Wise home, and they lived together long and happily.

## beats

- voyage: Through a storm Ivan Tsarevich rows to the island, and lightning shows the great oak with its hanging iron chest.
- bear: The bear he once spared tears the oak out by its roots, and the chest crashes down and bursts.
- hare: A grey hare bolts out and zigzags away, until Ivan's friend the hare runs it down in a cloud of fur.
- duck: A duck bursts from the hare into the storm, and the drake dives and strikes it in an explosion of feathers.
- egg: The glowing egg falls, bounces off the rocks and drops into the sea with a splash.
- pike: A huge pike leaps out of the waves with the egg in its jaws and lays it at Ivan's feet.
- koschei: Koschei, a skeletal sorcerer with a crown of green fire, rides down from the clouds and raises his sword over Ivan.
- needle: Ivan cracks the egg and bends the shining needle, and Koschei writhes in crackling green lightning.
- break: The needle snaps, Koschei crumbles to dust, his castle falls and the storm tears open on a golden sunrise.
- return: Ivan rows away at dusk, the oak rises with a new chest in its chains, and the storm gathers for Koschei, who never stays dead.
