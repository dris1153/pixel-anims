origin: France · Jules Verne's novel, 1870

## legend

In the year 1866, sailors all over the world spoke of a sea monster: something long and glowing, faster than any whale, that rammed ships in the dark. The American frigate Abraham Lincoln set out to hunt it. On board were Professor Aronnax, a French naturalist, his loyal servant Conseil, and Ned Land, a Canadian harpooner with a fierce temper and a sure arm.

After months of searching they found the creature shining on the sea at night. Ned hurled his harpoon, and it struck something hard. Then two huge spouts of water crashed over the deck, and Aronnax was flung into the sea, with Conseil and Ned soon beside him. They were saved not by the frigate but by the monster itself. It was an iron submarine called the Nautilus, and its master was Captain Nemo, a man who had broken with the whole of the land-dwelling world.

Nemo would never let them go, but he showed them the sea. The Nautilus ran on electricity. It had a library, a museum of wonders and a salon with great windows, where Nemo played his organ while fish glittered in the light outside. In heavy helmets, with tanks of air on their backs, the prisoners walked with him through undersea forests, hunting with electric guns. Near Ceylon, Nemo showed them a giant oyster holding a pearl as big as a coconut, and when a shark attacked a pearl diver, Ned Land killed it with his harpoon.

One night Nemo led Aronnax across the seabed to the ruins of Atlantis, lit red by an undersea volcano. The Nautilus sailed beneath the ice to the South Pole, and on the way back an overturning iceberg trapped it. For days the crew hacked at the ice and pumped out jets of boiling water to keep it from closing in, until the ship broke free just as the air ran out.

In the Atlantic, giant squid attacked. The crew fought them on the deck with axes, and one sailor was dragged into the sea. Nemo wept for him.

The captain grew darker. He sank a warship without mercy, and the prisoners knew they had to escape. Off the coast of Norway they slipped into the ship's little boat just as the Nautilus was drawn into the Maelstrom, the great whirlpool. Aronnax woke in a fisherman's hut in the Lofoten Islands. In less than ten months he had travelled twenty thousand leagues under the sea. Of Nemo and the Nautilus, no one ever heard again.

## beats

- hunt: At night a frigate hunts the sea monster on the surface, while below the Nautilus's lantern glows and it dives away.
- salon: The Nautilus swells close, and through its oval salon window Captain Nemo plays his organ among swirling fish.
- forest: Divers in round brass helmets walk the kelp forest with air tanks and electric guns as bubbles rise.
- pearl: A giant oyster opens on a glowing pearl, and a harpoon drives off the shark that lunges at the divers.
- atlantis: The sea darkens and the columns of Atlantis glow beside a volcano streaming lava.
- ice: Polar ice closes in and traps the ship, and boiling jets rise until the ice cracks open.
- squid: In a grey storm a giant squid grips the hull, sparks crackle through the plates, the crew swing axes and it flees in ink.
- maelstrom: The whole sea spins into the Maelstrom and the Nautilus is sucked down, spinning.
- calm: At dawn the whirlpool is gone and the ship rests on the seabed as bubbles rise to a glassy surface.
- dive: Its lantern glows again and it glides upward as night falls and the frigate returns to the hunt.
