## legend

Long ago, when the Ming army held the land of Đại Việt and taxed its people to the bone, a hero named Lê Lợi raised the banner of revolt in the forests of Lam Sơn. His men were brave but few, and battle after battle went against them. So the Dragon King, Long Quân, who watches over the people of this country, decided to lend them a sword.

A fisherman named Lê Thận cast his net into the river one night and drew up a strip of iron. He threw it back and cast again, and the same iron came up. A third time, and there it was again. Lê Thận held it to the lantern and saw that it was the blade of a sword. He kept it in a corner of his hut and, soon after, joined the army of Lam Sơn.

One day Lê Lợi came to that hut. In the dark corner the blade began to shine, and when he lifted it he found two words carved into the steel: Thuận Thiên, "by the will of Heaven". No one yet understood what it meant.

Later, fleeing the enemy through the forest, Lê Lợi saw a light at the top of a banyan tree. He climbed up and found a sword hilt set with jade. Remembering the blade, he brought the hilt back; the blade slid into it as if they had never been apart. Lê Thận knelt and offered the sword to his leader: "Heaven has placed its trust in you, my lord. We will follow you, flesh and bone, and this sword with us."

From that day the rebels grew bolder with every march. The sword blazed in battle, the Ming forts fell one after another, and at last the invaders were driven from the land. Lê Lợi took the throne and made Thăng Long his capital again.

A year later the king went boating on the Lake of Tả Vọng in the heart of the city. A Golden Turtle rose from the water and spoke: "Your Majesty, now that the land is at peace, return the sword to the Dragon King." The sword trembled at the king's belt. He held it out, and the turtle took it in its jaws and sank beneath the green water.

Ever since, that lake has been called Hoàn Kiếm, the Lake of the Returned Sword.

## beats

- lend: In the dawn mist the Dragon King moves as a golden shimmer under the lake and sends out two lights: the blade and the hilt.
- net: Lê Thận hauls up the glowing blade, the strip of iron that kept coming back to his net.
- hilt: Lê Lợi, with only two ragged followers, sees the light in the banyan and takes down the jade hilt.
- join: The fisherman offers the blade, it fits the hilt, and the words Thuận Thiên glint on the steel.
- rally: Lê Lợi raises the sword and soldiers with red banners stream in to join the rising.
- battle: The Lam Sơn army charges and the dark-armoured Ming soldiers break and flee.
- fort: The fort's black banners fall and red ones rise as the sun breaks through.
- crown: Thăng Long rises behind the trees, Lê Lợi is crowned, and a whole year turns in the sky.
- boat: The king rows out onto the lake in a dragon boat with the sword at his belt.
- ask: The Golden Turtle rises beside the boat and asks for the sword back.
- return: The sword glides into the turtle's jaws and the Dragon King's shimmer carries it home.
- named: Night on the Lake of the Returned Sword: lotus lanterns, the Turtle Tower and the red bridge of today.
