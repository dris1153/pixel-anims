## legend

Once a wicked hobgoblin made a mirror that shrank everything good and beautiful to nothing and made everything ugly look worse. His pupils flew it up toward heaven to mock the angels, but the higher they flew, the more it shook, until it slipped from their hands and shattered into millions of splinters. A splinter in the eye made a person see only what was wrong; a splinter in the heart turned it to a lump of ice.

In a big town lived two poor children, Kai and Gerda, in attics that faced each other across a gutter. Roses grew in boxes in the gutter, and in summer the two sat among them, as fond as brother and sister. Then one day a splinter flew into Kai's eye and another into his heart. He turned cold and mocking, tore the roses off and would not play with Gerda any more.

That winter Kai took his sled to the square, where a great white sleigh swept in, driven by someone wrapped in white fur. Kai tied his sled behind it, and away it flew out of the town while the snowflakes grew until they looked like big white hens. The driver was the Snow Queen, tall and dazzling white. She kissed him, so that he felt no cold and forgot Gerda and everyone at home.

In spring Gerda set out to find him. She wandered far, past an enchanted garden, a helpful crow, a prince and princess and a little robber girl, who gave her a reindeer to carry her north. At last the reindeer set her down in the snow, barefoot, near the Snow Queen's palace.

Its halls were drifted snow, lit by the northern lights. In the middle lay a frozen lake cracked into a thousand pieces, and there sat Kai, blue with cold though he did not feel it, pushing flat pieces of ice about to spell one word: Eternity. If he managed it, the Snow Queen had promised, he would be his own master, but he never could.

Gerda ran and hugged him, but he sat stiff and still. Then she wept, and her hot tears fell on his breast, reached his heart and melted the ice and the splinter in it. Kai burst into tears too and wept the splinter out of his eye. The pieces of ice danced for joy, and when they grew tired they lay down and spelled Eternity by themselves.

Hand in hand they walked home, and spring came with them. Back in their attics the roses were blooming. They had grown up, yet they were children still at heart, and it was summer, warm and glorious summer.

## beats

- mirror: The hobgoblin's pupils fly the magic mirror into the sky, it bursts, and splinters glitter down on Kai and Gerda among the rooftop roses.
- roses: A splinter strikes Kai's eye; he frowns and turns away, and the roses droop and drop their petals.
- sleigh: Winter falls, the Snow Queen sweeps into the square in her white sleigh, and Kai ties on his sled and is whirled away north.
- search: Gerda sets out barefoot across the snow with a crow flying beside her, and a reindeer bounds up to meet her.
- north: She rides the reindeer across the frozen plain while the aurora flares green and violet.
- palace: On the frozen lake Kai sits blue with cold, fitting shards of ice together, and Gerda runs to him.
- tear: Her warm tears fall, the splinter melts out of his eye in a flash, and the shards spell ETERNITY.
- spring: They walk home as the reindeer bounds away, the snow melts, and they sit among the blooming rooftop roses again.
