## legend

The demon king Ravana had carried off Sita, wife of Prince Rama, to his golden city of Lanka across the southern sea. The monkey armies searched the whole world for her, and at last they came to the shore of the ocean, where the land ends. No one could cross that water. No one, except Hanuman, son of the Wind, who had forgotten his own strength until his friends reminded him of it.

So Hanuman climbed the mountain by the shore and began to grow. He grew until the mountain groaned under his feet, animals fled its slopes and water burst from its rocks. Then he crouched, and leapt, and flew out over the sea like an arrow loosed from Rama's own bow.

Halfway across, the gods sent Surasa, mother of serpents, to test him. She rose from the waves and said no one could pass without entering her mouth. Hanuman grew larger, and she opened her jaws wider. He grew larger still, and her mouth grew wider still, as wide as a bay. Then, in a blink, Hanuman shrank to the size of a thumb, darted into her mouth and out again, and bowed to her. Surasa laughed, blessed him and let him go.

In Lanka he searched the palaces all night, and found Sita at last in the Ashoka grove, sitting under a tree, pale with grief. He knelt and gave her Rama's ring. When she saw it she wept for joy, and gave him her own jewel to carry back as a sign.

But Hanuman let himself be caught, so he could see Ravana face to face. The demon king ordered his tail wrapped in oily cloth and set on fire, to shame him. Hanuman grew, snapped his ropes and bounded from roof to roof with his tail blazing, until the golden city burned. Then he dipped his tail into the sea to cool it, and leapt home across the ocean with Sita's jewel in his hand, and the news that she was alive.

## beats

- crouch: On the mountaintop Hanuman grows until the mountain trembles, then crouches to spring.
- leap: He streaks across the ocean in a long arc as night falls.
- surasa: The sea serpent Surasa opens her jaws; he shrinks tiny, darts in and out, and flies on.
- grove: In the Ashoka grove he kneels before Sita and shows her Rama's ring.
- captive: Demon guards bind him and set his cloth-wrapped tail alight.
- blaze: He snaps the ropes and leaps from roof to roof, setting Lanka ablaze.
- douse: He dips his burning tail into the sea in a hiss of steam.
- return: He leaps back to the mountain with Sita's jewel as Lanka's fires fade.
