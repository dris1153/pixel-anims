## legend

At Chentang Pass, beside the East Sea, lived the general Li Jing and his wife, Lady Yin. She carried her third child for three and a half years, and when at last he was born, he came out as a ball of flesh. Li Jing split it with his sword, and out jumped a little boy with a golden ring on his wrist and a red silk sash around his middle. The immortal Taiyi Zhenren took the child as his pupil and named him Nezha.

When Nezha was seven, on a sweltering summer day, he went down to cool off where the river meets the sea. He dipped his red sash in the water and swished it back and forth. But the sash was a magic treasure: with every swish the sea turned red, and far below, the Crystal Palace of Ao Guang, Dragon King of the East Sea, shook on the seabed.

The Dragon King sent a yaksha up to find out what was wrong. The demon roared at the boy and attacked him, and Nezha threw his golden ring and struck him dead. Next came the Dragon King's third son, Ao Bing, with a halberd in his hand. Nezha caught him in the red sash, struck him down with the ring, and the prince fell back into his true shape, a small dragon, and lay still.

Ao Guang was wild with grief and fury. He gathered his brothers, the Dragon Kings of the other seas, and raised the water in a great flood against Chentang Pass. "Give us Nezha," they roared, "or everyone here will drown!"

Nezha would not let the people suffer for what he had done. "I did this," he said, "and I alone will answer for it." He gave his flesh back to his mother and his bones back to his father, and he gave up his life. The dragons were satisfied, and the waters fell back.

But his teacher would not let the story end there. Taiyi Zhenren laid out lotus flowers, lotus leaves and lotus roots in the shape of a boy, and called Nezha's spirit into them. Nezha rose again with a body of lotus, stronger than before. His teacher gave him the Fire-tipped Spear and a pair of Wind Fire Wheels that carried him through the air on flames.

Reborn, Nezha rode his wheels back to the East Sea and fought the Dragon Kings until they fled beneath the waves. And ever since, the lotus boy has been one of the most beloved heroes of Chinese legend.

## beats

- play: Little Nezha splashes in the shallows below the pass and swirls his red sash, and the waves begin to churn.
- quake: Far below, the Crystal Palace shakes, pearls roll, and the sea glows red wherever the sash sweeps.
- yaksha: A blue-faced yaksha bursts from the waves with a trident, and Nezha's golden ring knocks him back into the deep.
- prince: The sea heaves open and Ao Bing rises in silver armour on a dragon of water, halberd in hand.
- duel: Nezha's sash binds the water dragon, the ring strikes, and Ao Bing falls back into the sea as a small white dragon.
- flood: The sky turns to storm as the Dragon King Ao Guang rears over the horizon and drives a wall of water at Chentang Pass.
- sacrifice: Nezha stands on the wall and dissolves into red light and lotus petals, and the great wave falls back.
- lotus: A giant lotus rises from the sea, and its leaves and roots weave together at its heart into a new body.
- reborn: Nezha steps out on flaming Wind Fire Wheels with his Fire-tipped Spear and drives the Dragon King beneath the waves.
- return: At sunset the sea calms, the petals sink toward the palace, and the boy splashes in the shallows again.
