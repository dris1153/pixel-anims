## legend

Long ago, before the Hùng kings, there lived a lord of the dragon race named Lạc Long Quân. He was the son of Kinh Dương Vương and of the daughter of the Dragon Lord of Động Đình. He had the body of a dragon, and he lived mostly beneath the water, coming up onto the land only now and then. His strength had no equal, and he knew many kinds of magic.

In those days three demons tormented the people, and one of them lived in the East Sea. It was Ngư Tinh, a monstrous fish demon that had lived so long it could change its shape at will. It raised great waves to overturn the fishing boats and swallowed the people aboard. Soon no fisherman dared to put out to sea, and the villages along the coast went hungry.

Lạc Long Quân made up his mind to rid the people of it. He had a strong boat built, and he forged a great block of iron, heating it in the fire until it glowed red. Then he sailed straight toward the demon's lair, as though he were bringing it food.

Ngư Tinh rose from the deep and opened its enormous jaws to swallow the boat whole. At that moment Lạc Long Quân hurled the red-hot iron straight into its mouth. The demon howled and thrashed in agony, churning the sea to foam. Lạc Long Quân fought it among the waves and cut its body into three pieces. The old tellers say the head turned into a sea dog and was flung onto a mountain still called Cẩu Đầu, the Dog's Head, and the skin of the tail was stretched out to dry on an island still called Bạch Long Vĩ, the White Dragon's Tail.

With Ngư Tinh gone, the fishermen could sail again in peace. Lạc Long Quân went on to destroy the Fox Demon and the Tree Demon as well. He taught the people to farm, to raise animals and how to live in homes. Then he went back to his mother's palace beneath the water, and whenever the people needed him, he came again.

Later he married the fairy Âu Cơ, and from their hundred children came the Việt people, who still call themselves the children of the dragon and the fairy.

## beats

- watch: Lạc Long Quân stands on the cliff beside his fire at stormy dusk, watching the sea.
- rise: Ngư Tinh bursts out of the waves and snaps the fishermen's empty boat in half.
- forge: He thrusts a block of iron into the fire and breathes on it until it glows white-hot.
- hurl: As the demon lunges with its jaws wide, he hurls the burning iron straight into its mouth.
- dragon: He leaps from the cliff and becomes a long golden-green dragon, the true form of his race.
- cleave: The dragon coils around the demon and cuts it into three pieces with one lash of his tail.
- islands: The three pieces crash into the sea and harden into three rocky islets as the storm breaks.
- return: Mist hides the islets, the dragon becomes a man again by the fire, and a whole boat bobs at its mooring.
